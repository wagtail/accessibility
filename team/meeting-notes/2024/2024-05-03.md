  * Dataset of Wagtail website images and their alt text
  * Dataset is limited to only english content from not-too-commercial websites
  * OpenAI used to generate alt text for the images with this prompt
    * Please write a concise description of this image, 200 characters at most. Don’t start your description with generic phrases like 'a photo of' or 'a picture of', just describe. If the image contains legible text, your description has to also transcribe it. If the image is a logo or other text-only visual, only transcribe the text.
  * Albina and Thibaud have been scoring the original alt text and AI generated one.
  * Assumption is that AI is generally better, but we want that confirmed by this research
  * We have only reviewed 30% of the dataset but the conclusion so far is that AI is pretty good
  * AI is very capable of reading logo text or text printed on subjects in the image, like a canvas bag with writing on it.
  * Overall, AI is better than the average image alt text out there
